TROUBLESHOOTING:

Q: I don't see any logs with 🗺️
A: The screen might not be opening. Try:
   - Make sure you're actually on the Online map
   - Try: flutter logs | grep "OnlineWorldMapScreen"
   - Restart the app

Q: Logs are cut off
A: Scroll up in the terminal or save to file:
   - flutter logs > logs.txt

Q: App crashes when opening Online map
A: Check the logs for error messages and share them

Q: Still showing "Online: 0"
A: Check Supabase directly:
   SELECT user_id, is_online FROM user_activity WHERE user_id = 'YOUR_ID';
