## 2. What MatchMe is — and is not

**MatchMe connects people who are in the same place, or who will attend the same event, based on the kind of connection they are looking for and the interests they share.**

We match automatically, in the background. There is no browsing, no swiping, and no list of strangers to evaluate.

**What we are not:**

- **We are not a dating app.** People use MatchMe to meet in different ways — for romance, for friendship, as activity partners, and for professional contact. The Service does not steer you toward any one of these.
- **We are not a business network, a recruitment service, or a venue.**
- **We are not a replacement for your own judgement, and we do not verify that anyone is who they say they are.**

The goal of the Service is to help people meet in person. Whether you do, and what happens next, is entirely between you and the other person.

## Appendix — Drafting notes (remove before publication)

1. **Requires lawyer review before launch.** In particular sections 9 (payments and refunds), 11 (disclaimers and liability), and 14 (governing law and jurisdiction) are jurisdiction-specific and must be reviewed for every market you launch in.
2. **Section 9 — in-app purchases are currently disabled in code.** `paywall_screen.dart` has a hardcoded `_supportsNativePurchases => false`, and a "web test mode" path can grant a tier without payment. Do not publish terms describing a purchase flow that is not live, and fix that path before launch regardless of what the terms say.
3. **Section 11 and 5 — you are not a dating app, but the app currently presents paid features using dating-app language** ("see who likes you", "unlimited likes", "super likes", "rewind"), and no implementing code was found for those features. Either remove that copy or remove the promises.
4. **Section 6.2 and the Privacy Policy — the chat and profile content is encrypted on the device, but the service relies on an automated review of photographs**, which requires image data to leave the device. The terms and the policy must say the same thing.
5. **Section 7 — there is currently no in-app feature to share your live location with a trusted contact,** which the Privacy Policy and this section both imply is a good idea. Consider shipping it, and consider adding a short safety notice in the app itself rather than only in these documents.
6. **Section 3 — the 18+ check is client-side only,** with no database constraint. Add a server-side check, then this section is accurate rather than aspirational.
7. **Section 16 — "Language" clause assumes English governs.** If you localise these terms for Hebrew or Spanish under local law requirements, the governing-language clause will need to differ per locale.
8. **Fill in the contact addresses before publication.** Both are placeholders.
